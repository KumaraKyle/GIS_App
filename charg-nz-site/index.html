    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Will it work with my phone?

    Yes. Every powerbank has built-in USB-C, Lightning and Micro-USB cables, so it charges iPhone, Android, earbuds and most handheld devices.

    Do I need to download an app?

    No. Every station has a contactless card reader. Tap your card, phone or watch, pick your powerbank on the screen, and it unlocks.

    Can I return it somewhere else?

    Absolutely — that's the point. Drop it into any Charg-nz station with a free slot. You'll get a receipt as soon as it's returned.

    What if the station is full when I return?

    The station finder shows free slots in real time. If you arrive and it's full, contact us from the receipt link and we'll pause your rental while you find the next one.

    What if I lose it?

    If a powerbank isn't returned within 72 hours, it's yours to keep and a one-off purchase fee is charged. No further rental fees apply.
